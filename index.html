The Problem

The world is connected. Payments aren't.

Sending money across borders can still involve multiple intermediaries, currency conversions, high fees and settlement delays. For businesses operating internationally, these inefficiencies create friction that shouldn't exist in a digital economy.

01

Slow Settlement

Cross-border transfers can pass through several financial institutions before reaching the recipient.

02

High Costs

Intermediaries, foreign exchange spreads and transaction fees can make international payments expensive.

03

Fragmented Systems

Different currencies, banks and payment networks make moving value between countries unnecessarily complex.

The Solution

A new settlement layer for moving value across borders.

ValuePay is exploring blockchain and stablecoins as a settlement layer that can connect different currencies and payment systems, reducing the friction involved in international transactions.

01

Blockchain Settlement

Use blockchain rails to move value between participants without relying entirely on traditional correspondent banking.

02

Stablecoins

Use stablecoins as a digital settlement asset between different currencies and financial systems.

03

Local Connectivity

Connect blockchain settlement with local payment systems so users can send and receive money in familiar currencies.
